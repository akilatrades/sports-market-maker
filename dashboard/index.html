Test the mechanism. Report the failures.

Execution assumption. Trade-through fills and modeled fees do not capture the full exchange queue. The scaling study's 50% flow share is assumed, not measured.

Statistical limits. One NBA season, selected markets and correlated fills. ±2 reported standard errors are descriptive; live evaluation requires game-level uncertainty.

Cohort boundaries. The 21,373-fill model-veto replay and the 151-game sportsbook study use different samples. Their headline estimates are not a matched comparison.

Negative findings. The model-pick taker test lost 0.9¢ per contract after costs. Forecast information alone did not deliver an executable trading edge.

Metric definitions & reading guide
Order / fill / contract
An order can receive multiple fill events; each fill can contain multiple contracts. These counts answer different questions.
Settled P&L
Final contract payout minus entry cost and actual fees. Open position cost is not a realized loss.
Volume capture
Actual filled contracts divided by qualifying printed volume in comparable, fully observed resting-order windows.
Unsettled exposure
Outstanding orders and open positions are not included in the reported settled P&L.
Standard error
Uncertainty around an estimated mean. Correlated fills and selected experiments limit what a conventional error bar can establish.
06 / RISK & ENGINEERING

Constraints are part of the strategy.

Execution safeguards and an auditable record make the model testable under real operational limits.

Inspect the tests ↗
PRE-TRADE

Fresh inputs. Bounded exposure.

Projection age, availability checks and fee-aware edge filters precede order submission. Per-order, game, team and active-exposure limits constrain the NFL runner.

EXECUTION

Orders are not fills.

Resting quotes, cancellations and partial fills have distinct states. The account service is read-only; reporting does not submit orders.

MEASUREMENT

Keep a traceable research record.

Preserve rule versions, probability sources and timestamps. Missing outcomes and fees remain unknown; a checkpoint prompts review rather than automatic sizing.
