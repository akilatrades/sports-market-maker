The strategy in one paragraph

Retail traders tend to overpay for "YES" on a player exceeding a stat line. The strategy rests "NO" orders on the other side and lets the market come to it. Three steps separate an order from a profit: the order must fill, the game must settle, and exchange fees must be paid. Each step is tracked separately on this page.

Glossary
Order
An instruction to buy at a stated price. It can rest on the exchange without ever trading.
Fill
Part or all of an order has traded with another participant.
Settled
The game has finished and the contract has paid out, so the profit or loss is final.
Profit per contract
Average profit, in cents, on each contract after fees. The backtest estimate is 2.9¢.
Volume capture
Of all contracts that traded through the strategy's price, the share that filled its orders. Different from the fraction of a single order that filled.
Drawdown
The decline from a previous peak in cumulative profit, in dollars. It describes the losing stretches.
Standard error
A measure of how uncertain an average is. Error bars of ±2 standard errors that exclude zero indicate a result unlikely to be luck.
