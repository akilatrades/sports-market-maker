UNDERSTANDING THE STRATEGY

Buying NO is a position, not a locked-in profit.

The bot posts passive NO orders and holds filled contracts to settlement. A winning contract normally returns $1; a losing one returns $0. Profit depends on outcomes, entry prices, and actual fees. The 3¢ filter is an estimated advantage, not a promise.
